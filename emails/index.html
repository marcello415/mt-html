Rainin emails

The kinds of emails I built and sent at Rainin, rebuilt from mt.com/rainin assets on one modular template: a product launch, a free calendar, content promos from the Rainin Expertise Library, a webinar invite, hot and cold lead follow-ups and a service promo.

Where
Mettler-Toledo Rainin, as Web Producer
Emails
Promos, launches and lead follow-ups
My role
Template, content, audience, test and send
Tools
Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Content Builder

Product copy and images come from the archived SmartStand page (April 2019); the calendar email uses the 2017 calendar page; the Expertise Library emails use current mt.com pages and images. Subject lines, dates and the contact name are examples.

In the inbox

Eleven emails, one template

Every email uses the same header, footer and blocks: hero image, text, button, text and image, and an image grid. Only the content changes, so a new email could go from request to send quickly.
